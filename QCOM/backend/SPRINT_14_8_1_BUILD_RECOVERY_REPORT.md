## Root-cause corrections

- The payment registry and service now share the provider-neutral `PaymentProvider` contract. The legacy mock and Razorpay adapters retain legacy-only operations separately rather than making every registered provider implement them.
- Payment initiation uses `createPaymentSession()` and persists the provider reference; provider verification is called through the typed `verifyPayment()` contract. Disabled payment mode still fails closed for initiation.
- Refund status inquiry now models the status fields actually consumed. Optional idempotency-key lookup is represented in the neutral contract and implemented by the neutral mock.
- A provider refund reported as pending or requiring reconciliation remains `PROCESSING`; only `PROCESSED` transitions to completed. This preserves ambiguity and avoids treating an uncertain gateway outcome as a successful refund.
- The order service rejects absent delivery coordinates through an explicit validation error. The route already returns a client error for an absent/non-object location.
- Removed obsolete test fixture arguments/options and corrected stale import/JWT payload usage without deleting or skipping tests.
- No payment gateway calls were made; payment and refund behaviors use test providers.
